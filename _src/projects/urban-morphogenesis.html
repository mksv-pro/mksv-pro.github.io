~ Urban morphogenesis ~

Research project · Université Paris Cité · January 2026 · supervised by Raphael Raynaud

An agent-based model that couples Schelling's segregation dynamics with diffusion-limited aggregation (DLA), and studies the morphologies that emerge and their fractal dimension. An extension with reinforcement learning is under way.

The aggregation ingredient, live

Below, DLA alone (Witten and Sander, 1981). Each walker starts on a circle just outside the cluster, steps at random on a square lattice, and freezes at its first contact with the cluster; one that strays too far is released again. The fractal dimension D is read from the mass–radius law N(r) ∝ rD, fitted over the inner part of the cluster; in two dimensions it should settle near 1.71.
