~ Self-gravitating N-body systems ~

Research project · Sorbonne Université · February 2024 · supervised by Lionel Foret

Direct-integration simulation of gravitational N-body dynamics: every pair interaction is computed, with no tree or mesh approximation, to follow the collective effects that long-range forces produce.

Two systems, live

Both run on a kick–drift–kick leapfrog, a symplectic integrator, in units where G = 1. The figure eight is the periodic three-body orbit of equal masses found by Moore (1993) and proved to exist by Chenciner and Montgomery (2000); its initial conditions are Simó's. The cold collapse starts 200 bodies at rest, uniform in a sphere (shown in projection), with a softened force; the cloud falls in, rebounds and settles into a bound, roughly stationary state, the violent relaxation of Lynden-Bell (1967). The readout is the relative drift of the total energy, the integrator's own error bar.
